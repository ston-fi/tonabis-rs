# Third-party material

Catalog-derived interface layouts originate in TON Core's ABI catalog. The
original notice is retained in `LICENSE.ton-abi-catalog`. Source catalog metadata,
paths and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the binding inventory
`tools/reference/stonks/bindings.json`.

Rust bindings, tests and specifications retain the workspace proprietary policy.
Catalog metadata is not a license grant for captured executable cells. No executable
capture corpus, code-bearing sidecar or acquisition/replay scripts are distributed.
The bundled fixtures are independent code-free schema/synthetic encodings and
recorded scalar/address observations from the pinned source's safe fixture set.
